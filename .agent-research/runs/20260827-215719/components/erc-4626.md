# ERC-4626 strategy shares

Version: EIP-4626
Disposition: MISS-unversioned
Primary sources: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626 and https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/erc4626

## Verified facts

- ERC-4626 represents fungible shares of a vault with exactly one ERC-20 accounting asset.
- `totalAssets()` includes assets managed by the vault and compounding yield. Preview functions estimate the immediate transaction result but are not safe price oracles.
- OpenZeppelin documents the first-deposit/donation inflation attack and its virtual-share/virtual-asset and decimal-offset mitigation.
- The standard requires deposit/mint and withdraw/redeem accounting to reflect fees and slippage consistently.

## Assumptions and inferences

- A two-asset Delta position is not natively an ERC-4626 asset. A wrapper may accept one accounting asset and zap internally, but it must clearly disclose conversion, slippage, valuation, and an in-kind emergency exit extension.
- Per-NFT vaults can hold transferable strategy shares without sharing administrative custody or requiring 3,000 directly managed LP ranges.

## Risks

- **HIGH — Multi-asset semantic mismatch.** Calling a two-asset LP wrapper “standard ERC-4626” without a real single-asset redemption path produces misleading previews and unsafe integrations.
- **HIGH — Inflation and donation attacks.** A share vault without virtual accounting or seeded minimum liquidity can let an attacker dilute later deposits.

## Unresolved

- None for the standard. The product must choose whether Delta strategy shares are strict ERC-4626 wrappers or a custom ERC-20 share interface with explicit in-kind redemption.
