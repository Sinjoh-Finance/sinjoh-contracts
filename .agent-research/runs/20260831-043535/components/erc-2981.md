# Component: ERC-2981

**Date checked:** 2026-08-31
**Version:** Final
**Disposition:** MISS-new

## Facts

- ERC-2981 signals a receiver and percentage-derived amount; it does not enforce payment. Source: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2981
- A marketplace respecting ERC-2981 pays the royalty in the same unit as the sale price. Source: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2981
- The receiver owns any splitting and accounting complexity. Source: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2981

## Assumptions and inferences

- The collection royalty percentage belongs in immutable deployment configuration.
- The router must keep asset-specific accounting rather than assuming every royalty arrives as WETH.

## Risks

**HIGH — Hard-coded economic policy**

A source-level royalty percentage cannot represent per-collection economics and can disagree with marketplace configuration.

## Resolved questions

**Does ERC-2981 guarantee secondary revenue?**

No. Payment is voluntary and marketplace-dependent.
